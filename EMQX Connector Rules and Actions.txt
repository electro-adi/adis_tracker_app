Connection Rule:

SELECT
  connected_at,
  clientid
FROM
  "$events/client_connected"

Connection Action: Republish to topic "Tracker/events/connection" with qos 1

{
  "connected_at":${connected_at},
  "clientid":${clientid}
}


Disconnection Rule:

SELECT
  reason,
  disconnected_at,
  clientid
FROM
  "$events/client/disconnected"

Disconnection Action: Republish to topic "Tracker/events/disconnection" with qos 1

{
  "reason":${reason},
  "disconnected_at":${disconnected_at},
  "clientid":${clientid}
}
